## Current Feature

Fulfillment. Staff record full and partial shipments of confirmed orders; each shipment consumes the reservation and physical stock. After confirmation, retailers can request cancellation of all remaining quantities, and staff approve the whole request (which releases the reservation) or reject it with a reason. The order status is derived from the shipped and cancelled quantities (overview §5–§6).

## Status

Implemented and verified on `feature/fulfillment`. Waiting for the user's permission to commit.

## Goal

A confirmed order can be shipped in one or more shipments until nothing is outstanding. Alternatively, the retailer asks to cancel what has not shipped, and staff decide. Stock, reservations, movements, audit, and the order status stay consistent throughout, and the full order lifecycle works from draft to a terminal status.

## Scope and decisions (2026-09-30)

- Branch: `feature/fulfillment`.
- **Cancellation after confirmation** (overview §6 and §10 Phase 1):
  - The retailer requests cancellation of **all remaining unshipped quantities** in one request. Choosing lines or quantities is Phase 2.
  - Staff approve the whole request or reject it with a reason; there is no partial approval.
  - A pending request does not release stock and does not change the fulfillment status. Shipments may continue while a request is pending.
  - At approval, the quantities are rechecked: if a shipment has made the request ineligible, the response is 409 `CANCELLATION_CONFLICT` and nothing changes.
  - Only one pending request per order.
- **New statuses:** `partially_shipped`, `shipped`, `closed_partial`. `cancelled` can also be reached from `confirmed` when every unit is cancelled.
- **Terminal statuses:** `rejected`, `cancelled`, `shipped`, `closed_partial`.
- **Shipments:** staff choose a quantity per line, each at most the line's outstanding quantity. Shipments are immutable and uniquely numbered (`SH-001001`, generated by the database; lower numbers are reserved for the seed).
- **Out of scope:** carriers and tracking, returns, notifications, retailer selection of lines to cancel.

## Data and invariants

- **OrderLine:** cumulative `shippedQuantity` and `cancelledQuantity`, with `outstanding = quantity − shipped − cancelled ≥ 0` (CHECK). These are the only line columns that may change after submission, and only while the order is `confirmed` or `partially_shipped` (trigger).
- **StockReservation:** `quantityConsumed` equals the line's shipped quantity and `quantityReleased` equals its cancelled quantity. Checked in verification.
- **Shipment:** number, order, creator, and time. **ShipmentItem:** shipment, order line, and a quantity greater than 0. Both are append-only.
- **CancellationRequest:** order, status (`pending` | `approved` | `rejected`), requester and time, optional reason, decider and time, and a decision reason (required on rejection). **CancellationRequestItem:** request, order line, and quantity. Items are written when the request is created. At most one pending request per order (partial unique index).
- **Inventory movements:**
  - A shipment writes two movements per item, `shipment/sellable −q` and `shipment/reserved −q`, so available stock is unchanged.
  - An approved cancellation writes `release/reserved −q` per item, so available stock rises by q.
  - Both reference their reservation and the shipment or order number. The existing sum invariants (sellable, damaged, reserved) keep holding.
- **Order status data:**
  - `cancelled` after confirmation keeps its confirmation data. The approver is recorded as the canceller, and the request reason as the cancellation reason.
  - Shipment statuses keep submission and confirmation data.
- **Status derivation:** the trigger checks that every fulfillment status matches the derivation table computed from the line totals.
- **Migrations:** new enum values go in their own migration first.

## Consistency rules

- Every mutation (ship, request cancellation, approve, reject the request) requires an Idempotency-Key and the current order `version`. It runs Serializable with bounded retry and writes audit atomically, and it advances the order version.
- **Order of checks:**
  1. 404.
  2. `INVALID_ORDER_TRANSITION` (wrong status, or the order is terminal).
  3. `VERSION_CONFLICT`.
  4. 422 for invalid input.
  5. Business 409s: shipment quantity above outstanding → `SHIPMENT_QUANTITY_EXCEEDED`; request already pending → `CANCELLATION_REQUEST_PENDING`; nothing left to cancel → `INVALID_ORDER_TRANSITION`; approval no longer valid → `CANCELLATION_CONFLICT`.

## API contract

| Endpoint | Access | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/orders/:orderId/shipments` | Operator, administrator | `version`, `items[{orderLineId, quantity}]`; 200 with the updated order |
| `POST /api/orders/:orderId/cancellation-requests` | Retailer (own organization) | `version`, optional `reason`; creates a pending request for all outstanding quantities; 200 |
| `POST /api/orders/:orderId/cancellation-requests/:requestId/approve` | Operator, administrator | `version`; 200 |
| `POST /api/orders/:orderId/cancellation-requests/:requestId/reject` | Operator, administrator | `version`, `reason`; 200 |

- All four require CSRF and an Idempotency-Key.
- **Order responses add:**
  - per line: `shippedQuantity`, `cancelledQuantity`, `outstandingQuantity`;
  - `shipments` (number, time, actor, items with SKU and quantity);
  - `cancellationRequests` (status, reason, quantities, requester, decision).
- Status filters accept the new statuses.

## Audit

Order audit events `shipped` (shipment number and quantities per SKU), `cancellation_requested`, `cancellation_approved` (released quantities), and `cancellation_rejected` (reason).

## UI acceptance criteria

- **Staff, on a `confirmed` or `partially_shipped` order:**
  - A fulfillment table showing ordered, shipped, cancelled, outstanding, and reserved quantities per line.
  - A shipment form with a quantity per line (defaulting to the outstanding quantity; 0 skips the line) and **Record shipment**.
  - A pending cancellation request panel with the requested quantities and reason, plus **Approve** and **Reject** (reason required).
- **Retailer, on the same order:** the fulfillment table read-only, the shipments, and **Request cancellation of remaining items** (optional reason) when nothing is pending. A pending request is clearly shown.
- **All roles:** shipment history, request history, order history entries, and status badges with text for the new statuses.
- **Inventory:** shipment and release movements appear with readable labels.
- Keyboard access, a narrow layout, and `data-test` selectors following the contract.

## Verification

- **Access:** retailers cannot ship or decide; staff cannot request; unauthenticated callers and missing CSRF are rejected; other organizations get 404.
- **Shipments:**
  - Full and partial shipments; sellable and reserved drop equally while available stays the same; the reservation is consumed.
  - The status goes `confirmed` → `partially_shipped` → `shipped`.
  - Quantities above outstanding, zero or negative, unknown lines, lines from another order, and empty shipments are rejected without effects.
  - A replay applies nothing new.
- **Cancellation:**
  - Request creation; a second pending request is refused.
  - Rejecting the request changes nothing on the order's lines or stock.
  - Approving releases stock: `confirmed` → `cancelled` when nothing shipped, and `partially_shipped` → `closed_partial` when some units shipped.
  - A shipment between request and approval makes approval return `CANCELLATION_CONFLICT` with no effect.
- **Terminal orders:** no shipment, request, or decision is possible.
- **Concurrency:** parallel shipments of the last outstanding units never over-ship; a shipment racing an approval never double-counts.
- **Rollback:** an injected audit failure leaves no shipment, request decision, movement, or stock change.
- **Database:** constraints reject over-shipping, editing shipments or lines outside the rules, and a status that does not match the quantities.
- **Invariants:** movement sums; reserved equals the remaining reservations; consumed equals shipped and released equals cancelled.
- **Browser:**
  - Staff record partial then full shipments.
  - The retailer requests cancellation and staff approve (closed_partial) or reject it.
  - Inventory shows the movements; keyboard access, narrow layout, and isolation work.
- All existing checks pass with the updated seed. `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `pnpm build` pass.

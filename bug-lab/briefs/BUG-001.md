# Brief BUG-001: fulfillment status

**Area:** shipping confirmed orders (staff) and the order status that retailers and staff see.

**Rule to verify (overview §5):** after confirmation, the order status is derived from the quantities:
- nothing shipped yet → `confirmed`;
- something shipped and something outstanding → `partially_shipped`;
- nothing outstanding and nothing cancelled → `shipped`;
- nothing outstanding, some shipped and some cancelled → `closed_partial`.

**Setup:** `pnpm bug-lab setup --defect BUG-001`, then `pnpm bug-lab start --run …`. Also prepare a `none` run to compare.

**Suggested charter:** as `operator@pandora.test`, ship confirmed orders in one shipment and in several. Look at:
- the status badge;
- the ordered, shipped, and outstanding quantities;
- which actions are still offered, to staff and to the retailer;
- the shipment history.
